Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Ranked is still in development and isn’t available yet. This policy describes how the app will handle your information when it launches, and it will be updated before release.

What Ranked collects

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple or Google, Ranked receives your name, your email address and, if available, your profile photo.
  • Your profile. Your username, display name, an optional bio and photo, and your date of birth, which is used only to confirm you’re 13 or older.
  • What you add. Your ranked lists, the notes you write on titles, your Up Next list, and the people you follow.
  • Invites. If you joined through a friend’s invite link, which invite you used.

Ranked doesn’t use advertising, doesn’t sell your information, and doesn’t use third-party analytics or tracking.

Who can see what

  • Your circle, the people you follow who also follow you back, can see your ranked lists, your notes, and your ranking activity.
  • Other Ranked users can see your username, display name and photo, so friends can find you.
  • Only you can see your Up Next list and your date of birth.

Services Ranked relies on

  • Supabase stores your account and everything you add, and handles sign-in.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them.
  • TMDB and Google Books provide title information. When you search, your search text is sent to them.
  • The App Store and Google Play handle any purchases. Ranked never sees your payment details.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from Settings in the app. That permanently deletes your profile, your lists, your notes, your Up Next list and your connections. Titles in Ranked’s shared catalog (names, years and cover images from TMDB or Google Books) aren’t personal data and stay in the catalog.

Children

Ranked is for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, let us know through the support page and it will be deleted.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above will change too, and significant changes will be announced in the app. For questions, use the support page.
