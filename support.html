Support

Ranked is in development for iPhone and Android.

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on Ranked’s GitHub page. Say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Issues there are public, so don’t include your email address or other personal details.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your lists, notes and connections. The privacy policy explains exactly what’s removed.

Can’t find a title?

Ranked’s catalog comes from TMDB for films and TV, and from Google Books and Open Library for books. Try a different spelling, or check that you’ve picked the right medium (Books, Films or TV) above the search results.

Film and TV data from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Book data from Google Books and Open Library.
